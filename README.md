# Atlas *(Working Title)*

> **Instagram is where creators publish.**
> **Atlas is where creators run their business.**

## What is Atlas?

Atlas is a platform that helps content creators manage everything that happens behind the scenes.

Making content is only one part of being a creator. Every day, creators also have to reply to brand emails, keep track of payments, manage contracts, remember deadlines, plan content, and organize files. Most people use different apps for each of these tasks, which makes things messy and time-consuming.

Atlas aims to bring all of this into one place.

The long-term goal is to build a workspace where creators can manage their business while AI helps them stay organized, find information quickly, and make better decisions.

---

# Why am I building this?

As I started creating content and working with brands, I realized that managing everything was becoming harder than creating the content itself.

Important emails get buried.

Payments are easy to forget.

Contracts are scattered across folders.

Deadlines are tracked in different apps.

Every creator ends up building their own system using spreadsheets, notes, calendars, and reminders.

I believe there should be a better way.

---

# The Goal

Build a platform that helps creators spend less time managing their business and more time creating content.

---

# The Vision

Atlas is not just another productivity app.

The idea is to create one place where creators can manage everything related to their work.

Instead of switching between multiple apps, creators should be able to see everything in a single workspace.

Over time, Atlas will become smart enough to understand the connections between brands, campaigns, payments, contracts, content, and performance.

---

# Planned Features

The project will grow step by step.

Some of the features planned for the future include:

* Brand and collaboration management
* Campaign tracking
* Payment and invoice tracking
* Contract management
* Content planning
* Deadline management
* Analytics
* AI-powered search
* Smart reminders
* Workflow automation

Not all of these will be built at once. The project will start small and expand over time.
