# User Research

## Purpose

The purpose of this document is to understand the problems faced by content creators when managing the business side of their work.

The research focuses on identifying common workflows, pain points, and opportunities where Atlas can provide meaningful value.

---

# Target Users

The initial target audience for Atlas includes:

- Individual content creators
- Nano creators (1K–10K followers)
- Micro creators (10K–100K followers)
- Freelancers working with brands
- UGC (User-Generated Content) creators

Future versions may expand to:

- Creator teams
- Talent managers
- Agencies
- Businesses hiring creators

---

# Current Creator Workflow

Most creators use multiple tools throughout a collaboration.

### Typical Workflow

1. Receive an email or Instagram DM from a brand.
2. Discuss campaign details.
3. Negotiate pricing.
4. Agree on deliverables and deadlines.
5. Create and submit content.
6. Send an invoice.
7. Wait for payment.
8. Store contracts and campaign assets.

Each step often happens in a different application.

---

# Pain Points

## 1. Scattered Information

Creators switch between Gmail, Google Drive, Notion, Google Sheets, Calendar, and messaging apps to manage one collaboration.

---

## 2. Payment Tracking

Many creators manually track:

- Invoice dates
- Payment due dates
- Payment status

Missing a payment follow-up can lead to delayed income.

---

## 3. Campaign Management

As collaborations increase, creators struggle to remember:

- Which content has been submitted
- Pending approvals
- Deadlines
- Deliverables

---

## 4. Brand Relationship Management

Past conversations, pricing history, and previous campaigns are difficult to find quickly.

---

## 5. Manual Administration

A significant amount of time is spent on administrative work instead of creating content.

---

# User Goals

Creators want to:

- Stay organized
- Never miss deadlines
- Track payments easily
- Find information quickly
- Spend more time creating content

---

# Atlas Opportunities

Atlas can improve creator workflows by:

- Centralizing campaign information
- Tracking payments automatically
- Organizing brand relationships
- Managing deliverables
- Providing a single workspace for creator operations
