# Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

## Purpose

The first version of Atlas is not meant to solve every problem creators face.

Its goal is to solve one important problem exceptionally well and provide a strong foundation for future development.

---

## Target Users

The initial version is built for:

- Individual content creators
- Freelancers who work with brands
- Small creators managing collaborations themselves

The MVP will not support creator teams or agencies.

---

## Problem Statement

Creators often use multiple apps to manage brand collaborations, payments, deadlines, and communication.

As the number of collaborations grows, it becomes difficult to remember:

- Which brands have replied
- Which invoices are pending
- Upcoming deadlines
- Campaign status
- Previous conversations

Atlas aims to centralize this information into one workspace.

---

## MVP Goals

The first version should allow creators to:

- Manage brands
- Track collaborations
- Monitor campaign progress
- Track payments
- Store contracts and important files
- View all creator business information in one place

---

## Core Features

### Authentication

- Secure user accounts
- User profiles

---

### Brand Management

- Add brands
- Store contact information
- Track communication
- View collaboration history

---

### Campaign Management

- Campaign details
- Deliverables
- Deadlines
- Status tracking

---

### Payment Tracking

- Expected payment
- Payment status
- Invoice status
- Payment history

---

### Dashboard

A simple dashboard showing:

- Active campaigns
- Upcoming deadlines
- Pending payments
- Recent activity

---

## What is NOT included

The MVP will not include:

- AI recommendations
- Advanced analytics
- Team collaboration
- Mobile app
- Multi-platform integrations
- Accounting features
- Content scheduling

These will be considered after validating the core product.

---

## Success Criteria

The MVP is successful if creators can manage their collaborations without relying on multiple spreadsheets or note-taking apps.

---

## Future Expansion

Once the MVP is validated, Atlas will expand into:

- AI-powered creator memory
- Smart search
- Analytics
- Workflow automation
- Financial insights
- Creator intelligence
