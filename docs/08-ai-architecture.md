# AI Architecture

## Purpose

Atlas aims to evolve from a management platform into an intelligent assistant that helps creators organize, retrieve, and understand their business information.

---

# AI Goals

The AI system should:

- Understand creator workflows
- Retrieve information quickly
- Reduce manual searching
- Assist with business decisions

---

# Phase 1

No AI.

Focus on collecting structured data.

---

# Phase 2

Natural language search.

Examples:

"Show unpaid campaigns."

"Which brands contacted me this month?"

---

# Phase 3

Creator Memory

The AI remembers:

- Previous campaigns
- Brand preferences
- Payment history
- Meeting notes

---

# Phase 4

Recommendations

Examples:

- Follow up with Brand X.
- Payment overdue by 10 days.
- Similar campaigns generated the highest revenue.

---

# Future AI Features

- AI Chat Assistant
- Proposal generation
- Meeting summaries
- Smart reminders
- Contract summarization
- Revenue forecasting

---

# Technologies

- LLM APIs
- Embeddings
- Vector Database
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- FastAPI AI services

---

# Guiding Principle

AI should enhance creator workflows rather than replace human decision-making. Every AI feature must save time, improve organization, or surface useful insights.
