# Product Roadmap

## Purpose

This roadmap outlines the long-term vision for Atlas.

Rather than building every feature at once, Atlas will grow in phases. Each phase delivers value while laying the foundation for future capabilities.

---

# Phase 1: Foundation (MVP)

### Goal

Help creators manage their business from one place.

### Features

- User authentication
- Creator profile
- Brand management
- Campaign tracking
- Deliverables management
- Payment tracking
- Contract and file storage
- Dashboard with active work and deadlines

### Success Criteria

A creator can manage all their brand collaborations without relying on spreadsheets or multiple note-taking apps.

---

# Phase 2: Smart Workspace

### Goal

Make Atlas more intelligent and reduce manual work.

### Features

- Global search
- Smart filters
- AI-assisted notes
- Automatic reminders
- Campaign templates
- Calendar integration
- Email integration
- Notifications

### Success Criteria

Creators spend less time managing work and more time creating content.

---

# Phase 3: AI Creator Assistant

### Goal

Transform Atlas into an intelligent assistant instead of just a management tool.

### Features

- AI Creator Memory
- Natural language search
- Meeting summaries
- Brand conversation history
- AI-generated follow-ups
- Intelligent task suggestions
- Campaign insights

### Success Criteria

Creators can ask Atlas questions such as:

- "Which brands haven't paid me?"
- "When did I last work with Nike?"
- "Show campaigns due this week."

---

# Phase 4: Creator Intelligence

### Goal

Help creators make better business decisions.

### Features

- Revenue analytics
- Brand performance reports
- Campaign ROI
- Pricing insights
- Collaboration history
- Income trends
- Monthly business reports

### Success Criteria

Atlas becomes the creator's business dashboard.

---

# Phase 5: Creator Operating System

### Goal

Become the central workspace for running a creator business.

### Features

- Team collaboration
- Agency support
- Multi-platform integrations
- Workflow automation
- AI-powered recommendations
- Brand relationship management
- Financial forecasting

### Success Criteria

Creators can run their entire business from Atlas.

---

# Future Ideas

Potential features for future exploration include:

- Mobile application
- Invoice generation
- Tax reports
- Public media kit
- Proposal generator
- Brand marketplace
- AI pricing recommendations
- Voice assistant
- Chrome extension
- API for third-party integrations

---

# Guiding Principles

Atlas will be developed according to these principles:

- Solve one problem well before expanding.
- Build features based on creator feedback.
- Prioritize simplicity over complexity.
- Use AI only where it adds real value.
- Maintain a scalable and modular architecture.

---

# Vision

Atlas aims to become the operating system for creators by providing a single, intelligent workspace that brings together brand management, campaigns, payments, files, analytics, and AI assistance.
